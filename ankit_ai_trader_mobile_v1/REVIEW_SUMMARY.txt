ANKIT AI TRADER — PHASE 1 + TRADE ALERT NOTIFICATIONS SUMMARY

1. ADDED `lib/services/trade_alert_formatter.dart`:
   - Formats ENTRY, EXIT, and SAFETY LOCK notifications clearly labeled with `PAPER TRADE` / `[PAPER TRADE]`.
   - Entry alert includes symbol, side, entry price, quantity, stop-loss, take-profit, and dollar amount risked.
   - Exit alert includes symbol, exit price, reason, net P&L after fees/slippage, and new account equity.

2. UPDATED `lib/services/persistence_db.dart`:
   - Persists `tradeAlertsEnabled` (default `true` / ON) in `engine_kv_state`.
   - Persists `notifiedAlertIds` and `lastNotifiedOrderId` in `engine_kv_state` to guarantee at-most-once alert delivery per order/lock event across service restarts.

3. UPDATED `lib/services/authoritative_engine.dart`:
   - Authoritative background engine checks `persistence.getTradeAlertsEnabled()` and `persistence.markAlertIdNotified(alertId)` before dispatching each alert over the background method channel.
   - Emits alerts for new BUY entries, SELL exits, Daily Loss Limit lock, $90 Max Drawdown / Equity Floor lock, and Internet Loss Pause.

4. UPDATED `TradingForegroundService.kt` & `MainActivity.kt`:
   - Added separate `NotificationChannel("trade_alerts_channel", "Trade Alerts", NotificationManager.IMPORTANCE_HIGH)` with sound/heads-up banner and unique dismissible notification IDs (`>= 2000`), never overwriting `SERVICE_NOTIFICATION_ID` (`1101`).
   - Added safe Android 13+ `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` permission check and `openNotificationSettings` intent handler.

5. UPDATED `lib/services/trading_coordinator.dart` & `lib/main.dart`:
   - Added `"Trade alert notifications"` ON/OFF switch on the dashboard backed by SQLite, plus a non-crashing permission warning banner and `"NOTIFICATION SETTINGS"` button when notifications are blocked.

6. UPDATED `test/phase1_trading_test.dart` & `test/widget_test.dart`:
   - Added unit tests (Tests 22–25) for entry/exit alert formatting, SQLite toggle persistence, no alert when toggle is OFF while foreground notification stays active, and duplicate order-ID alert prevention.
   - Total automated tests: 26 (25 unit/persistence/integration tests + 1 widget test).
